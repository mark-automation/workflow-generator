    How the engine works

    Five stages, one webhook, zero manual touch until money is on the table.

    Capture

    Any form, ad platform or chatbot POSTs to one webhook URL. Nothing gets missed.

    Enrich

    Domain intelligence + source weighting — corporate vs free mail, paid vs organic.

    Score

    LLM grades intent 0–100 against your deal-breakers: budget, urgency, fit. Rules fallback if the API blinks.

    Route

    Hot → Slack ping + SMS-grade alert. Warm → daily digest. Cold → nurture sequence. You only see what matters.

    Reply & book

    A personalized first-touch email with your calendar link lands in their inbox — logged to Sheets for the record.
